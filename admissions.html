Admission Requirements

OPEN ADMISSIONS

General & Programme Entry Requirements

Admissions open to national and international candidates without discrimination.

Admissions to UTAMU are open to all qualified national and international applicants. A candidate is admitted when they meet the required criteria under the guidance of the National Council for Higher Education (NCHE) and approval by the Senate.

  • Bachelors
  • Masters & PGD
  • Diplomas
  • PhD
  • International

Undergraduate Degree Admission Pathways:

  • 1. Direct Entry: At least two (2) Principal Passes at the same sitting in the Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) or its equivalent. (For BSc Computer Science/Engineering/Software Eng: At least a Principal Pass in Mathematics or Physics).
  • 2. Diploma Scheme: At least a Second Class (Lower Division) Diploma in a relevant computing, business, legal or scientific field from a recognized institution.
  • 3. Cisco CCNA / Professional Entry: Cisco Certified Network Associate certificate plus at least 1 Principal and 2 Subsidiary passes in UACE.

Masters & Postgraduate Diploma Entry Requirements:

  • 1. Masters Programmes: A Bachelor's degree with at least Lower Second Class honors (or its equivalent) from a recognized degree-awarding institution, OR a Postgraduate Diploma in the same field.
  • 2. Postgraduate Diplomas (PGD): A Bachelor's degree or recognized professional qualification in any discipline.

Ordinary Diploma Entry Requirements:

  • At least five (5) passes in the Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) at the same sitting; AND
  • At least one (1) Principal Pass and two (2) Subsidiary Passes obtained at the same sitting in UACE.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) Entry:

  • An Honors Bachelor's degree and a Master's degree from recognized institutions;
  • A viable PhD concept paper defended orally before the doctoral panel;
  • Curriculum Vitae of proposed supervisor(s).

International & Transfer Applicants:

  • Foreign certificates equated by the Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB) or National Council for Higher Education (NCHE);
  • Credit transfer available for qualifying courses taken at other accredited universities.
