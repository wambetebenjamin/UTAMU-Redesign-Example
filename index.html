WELCOME TO UTAMU

Experiential Learning For An Open Mind

Infusing 21st-century technology and management sciences to empower tomorrow's creators.

Universal Technology and Management University (UTAMU) is a premier private university established under the National Council for Higher Education in March 2013 (Gazette Vol. CVI No. 14). UTAMU combines management and technology in all its programmes to equip graduates with critical thinking, problem solving, communication, collaboration, and creativity.

Applied E-Learning & E-Teaching

Our state-of-the-art e-learning platform delivers interactive 24/7 digital course content that enables working professionals and campus students to learn seamlessly anywhere.

Distinguished Scholar-Practitioners

Courses are taught by a rich blend of seasoned professors and active industry experts (Practice Lecturers to Practice Professors) bridging academic theory and real-world practice.

Innovation, Research & Incubation

UTAMU creates opportunities through our Innovation Hub, annual ICTM international conference, and peer-reviewed IJOTM journal publication series.
