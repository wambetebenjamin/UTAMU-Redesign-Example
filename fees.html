Fees and Tuition Payment

TRANSPARENT TUITION STRUCTURE

University Fees & Bank Payment Procedures

Approved by the UTAMU University Council

Tuition and fees are charged per semester. All fees must be paid through authorized bank accounts or mobile money channels. No university staff member is permitted to accept cash payments.

Application Fees

  • Undergraduate, PGD & Masters: UGX 50,000 (EAC) / USD 30 (International).
  • PhD Programmes: UGX 100,000 (EAC) / USD 100 (International).
