Functions of Council

  • Formulate the general academic and administrative policies;
  • Implement decisions of the Board of Directors;
  • Approve university budgets, expenditures and financial accounts;
  • Appoint academic, administrative, and technical staff;
  • Ensure staff and student welfare, discipline, and code of conduct.

Standing Committees of Council

1. Appointments & Disciplinary Committee: Responsible for staff recruitment, promotion, welfare, and disciplinary procedures.

2. Finance, Administration and General-Purpose Committee: Responsible for financial monitoring, salary structures, and resource allocation.

3. Audit and Risk Committee: Oversees internal controls, risk management framework, and statutory audit compliance.

4. Quality Assurance Committee: Monitors institutional benchmarks and academic standards.
