FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know About UTAMU

What is UTAMU and how long has it been in existence?

Universal Technology And Management University (UTAMU) was incorporated in August 2012 and licensed by the National Council for Higher Education (NCHE) on 11th March 2013 (License No. UIPL022, Uganda Gazette Vol. CVI No. 14). UTAMU offers experiential, blended learning in technology, management, law, and education for 21st-century learners.

Are UTAMU degrees accredited and recognized internationally?

Yes. All academic programmes offered at UTAMU are accredited by the National Council for Higher Education (NCHE) of Uganda. Degrees, diplomas, and certificates awarded by UTAMU are recognized globally as official Ugandan higher education awards.

What study modes and intakes are available?

UTAMU runs 3 triannual intakes every academic year: January, May, and September. Learning options include Day, Evening, Weekend, and full Distance/Virtual E-Learning modes to suit both campus students and working professionals.

How does the UTAMU blended learning model work?

UTAMU utilizes an interactive online e-learning management system combined with physical classroom sessions. Lectures, course materials, assignments, discussions, and e-library databases are accessible 24/7 online, supplemented with block-release interactive sessions.

Universal Technology And Management University (UTAMU) is a premier private university licensed by the National Council for Higher Education (NCHE) in Uganda. Dedicated to technology, management, and research for an open mind.
