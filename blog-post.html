Call for Sponsorship and Invitation to attend ICTM 2026

By UTAMU Secretariat September 10, 2026 Conferences & Events

The Organizing Committee of the International Conference on Technology and Management 2026 (ICTM 2026) invites the general public, academic researchers, private sector partners, and industry innovators to sponsor and participate in the upcoming ICTM 2026.

ICTM 2026 will take place from 21st to 23rd September 2026 at the UTAMU Kungu Campus in a hybrid format under the theme “Responsible Artificial Intelligence for Sustainable Digital Transformation.”

"We believe this conference will provide a valuable global platform for advancing research, technological innovation, and multi-sectoral collaboration in technology and management."

Key Conference Tracks:

  • Track 1: Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning & Ethical Frameworks
  • Track 2: Cybersecurity, Data Privacy and Legal Informatics
  • Track 3: Digital Transformation in Business, Supply Chain & Governance
  • Track 4: Technology in Pedagogy & Healthcare Delivery

For sponsorship packages, keynote registration, and paper submissions, please contact: ictm@utamu.ac.ug

Thank you for your continued support of UTAMU.
