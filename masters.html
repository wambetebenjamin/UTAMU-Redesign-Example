Distinguished Masters Programmes

Equipping corporate executives, researchers, legal experts, and technologists.

Universal Technology And Management University (UTAMU) offers uniquely designed Masters programmes with a wide range of specialisation options for learners. All programmes are taught by a rich blend of distinguished professors and industry experts and give special focus to case studies and practical teaching.

Admission Requirements

  • A Bachelor's degree with at least a Lower Second Class honors (or its equivalent) from a recognized degree-awarding institution; OR
  • A recognized Postgraduate Diploma in a relevant field; OR
  • A recognized professional qualification equivalent to a Bachelor's degree.
