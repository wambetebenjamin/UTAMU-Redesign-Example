General Admission Requirements

  • Direct Entry: At least two (2) Principal Passes at the same sitting in UACE (Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education) or its recognized equivalent.
  • Diploma Holders: At least a Second Class (Lower Division) Diploma from an accredited tertiary institution.
  • Professional / Cisco CCNA: Recognized Cisco CCNA or professional qualification with at least 1 Principal pass and 2 Subsidiaries in UACE.
