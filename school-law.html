UTAMU School of Law

LEGAL SCHOLARSHIP & ADVOCACY

UTAMU School of Law

Twinned Foundations of Excellence, Humanity, and Practical Legal Practice

Landmark MoU with Uganda Law Society (ULS)

UTAMU and Uganda Law Society have forged a strategic partnership to advance legal education, professional training, continuing legal education, and the infusion of AI in jurisprudence.

Message from the Dean

"It is an absolute privilege to lead one of the best law schools. UTAMU Law School is a deeply values-driven school, where excellence and humanity are twinned at the heart of our foundations. UTAMU Law School is well-positioned to address the major challenges of our time, from the rapid technological advances transforming legal practice to the heightened polarization straining legal and political institutions."

Our Bachelor of Laws (LLB) curriculum integrates practical experience, professional responsibility, and lawyering skills with doctrinal study at every level: problem-solving, legal analysis, oral argument, negotiation, mediation, litigation, and ethical decision-making.

Specialized Law Centres

Clinical Legal Education

Supporting students to learn substantive law and practice in the context of real cases and community legal clinics.

Moot Court Centre

Simulation courtroom training for national and regional moot court competitions and trial advocacy skills.

ICT and Law Centre

Researching artificial intelligence in legal practice, data privacy, cyber law, and intellectual property.

Environmental & Natural Resources

Policy research and advocacy in mining law, oil & gas regulations, carbon markets, and sustainability.
