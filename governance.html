Governance Structure

UNIVERSITY GOVERNANCE

Statutory Governance & Administrative Structure

Fostering transparency, accountability, and academic excellence

Universal Technology and Management University (UTAMU) operates under a robust, legally structured governance framework in accordance with the Universities and Other Tertiary Institutions Act, 2001 (as amended), the UTAMU Charter, and statutory regulations.

Supreme Organ

Board of Directors

Holds the university in trust on behalf of shareholders, provides strategic oversight, and appoints key university officers.

View Board Details »
Titular Head

The Chancellor

Titular head of UTAMU who presides over convocations, confers all degrees and academic distinctions, and promotes the university globally.

Chancellor's Profile »
Policy & Administration

University Council

Responsible for overall policy formulation, strategic directions, financial management, staff appointments, and operational oversight.

Supreme Academic Organ

University Senate

In charge of teaching standards, curriculum approval, research direction, examinations, quality assurance, and academic awards.

Senate Details »
Executive Execution

University Management

Led by the Vice Chancellor, Deputy Vice Chancellors, University Secretary, Academic Registrar, and School Deans responsible for the day-to-day operations and academic delivery of the University.
