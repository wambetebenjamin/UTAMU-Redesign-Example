STUDENT ENROLLMENT GUIDE

UTAMU Joining Instructions & Orientation

Guidelines for newly admitted Undergraduate and Graduate learners.

Congratulations upon your admission to Universal Technology and Management University! Please review the step-by-step checklist below to complete your registration, verification, and orientation.

Registration & Enrollment Steps

Step 1: Pick Up or Download Your Admission Letter

Pick up your official admission letter from the UTAMU Student Resource Centre (Bugolobi Campus) or download it from the online application portal.

Step 2: Payment of Tuition and Functional Fees

Pay your tuition and functional fees to any of the designated UTAMU bank accounts or via Mobile Money SchoolPay.

Step 3: Document Verification

Present original academic certificates, transcripts, identity documents, and 2 passport photos to the Academic Registrar's office for verification.

Step 4: E-Learning Portal & E-Library Activation

Receive your student institutional email and login credentials for the UTAMU E-Learning Platform and digital library databases.
